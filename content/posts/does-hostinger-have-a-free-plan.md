## TL;DR

- **No, not for web hosting.** Hostinger's help centre says standard hosting plans "do not include an entirely free tier" ([Hostinger help centre](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583320-are-free-hosting-plans-available-at-hostinger/), 13 October 2026).

- **What it offers instead:** a 30 day money-back guarantee on new hosting plans, a 7 day trial of its AI Builder (a separate product), and a free domain for the first year on yearly or longer plans.

- **The lowest priced Websites plan, Premium, is $2.99 a month on a 48 month term** ($143.52 upfront) and renews at $10.99 a month ([Hostinger pricing](https://www.hostinger.com/pricing), 13 October 2026).

- **A refund deletes the site.** Files, databases and emails are permanently deleted when you submit the request, so back up first.

No. Hostinger has no free plan for web hosting. What it calls free is a refund window, a trial of a different product and a domain. If a bill has ever come in higher than the plan you thought you bought, don't judge this by the [$2.99 promo rate](https://www.hostinger.com/pricing) (read 13 October 2026). The price section below is the part to read. Plan detail is in our [Hostinger review for freelancers](/reviews/hostinger-review-freelancers-2026/).

## What does Hostinger offer instead of a free plan?

It offers three things. None is a free hosting plan.

1. **A 30 day money-back guarantee** on new web, cloud, agency, KVM and Game Panel hosting plans and Hostinger Business Mail ([help centre](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583320-are-free-hosting-plans-available-at-hostinger/)).

2. **A 7 day AI Builder trial.** It "lasts 7 days", and "websites cannot be published" during it ([trial page](https://www.hostinger.com/support/11131062-hostinger-ai-builder-how-to-try-it-for-free/)). It is a separate product from hosting.

3. **A free domain for one year** with a yearly or longer plan. Hostinger says "not all TLDs are eligible", and the domain "must be registered within 12 months of acquiring the hosting plan, or it will be cancelled" ([Hostinger pricing](https://www.hostinger.com/pricing)).

## How does the 30 day guarantee work as a trial?

You pay first, then ask for the money back. The refund policy says products "may be refunded only if canceled within 30 days of the date of the transaction" ([refund policy](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/refund-policy)). Hosting is on the refundable list. Domain renewals, privacy protection and AI Builder credits are not, and cryptocurrency payments are not refunded. The help centre recommends a credit card or e-wallet for faster refunds.

Two limits catch people out:

- **The refund cancels the service.** Hostinger says your files, databases and emails "will be deleted permanently" once you submit, and the request is "not reversible" ([refund how-to](https://www.hostinger.com/support/3958999-how-to-get-a-refund-at-hostinger/)). Download backups first.

- **Refunds are not unlimited.** The [refund policy](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/refund-policy) lets Hostinger "unilaterally decline the request for a refund" if it sees abuse. Domain registrations also sit outside the guarantee and "have separate, restricted refund terms" ([help centre](https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583320-are-free-hosting-plans-available-at-hostinger/)).

## Is there genuinely free hosting for a website?

Only for static files. Cloudflare Pages on its Free plan allows 500 builds a month. Each site can hold 20,000 files, and a single file can be 25 MiB ([Cloudflare limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/), 13 October 2026). Cloudflare's [static WordPress guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/how-to/deploy-a-wordpress-site/) turns a WordPress site into static files and lists forms, comments and `/wp-admin` links as unsupported.
