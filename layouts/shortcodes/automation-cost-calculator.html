What does not count as a billable action

Zapier does not bill the trigger, and it does not bill its own built-in tools: Formatter, Paths, Filter, Delay, Looping, Sub-Zap, Digest, Zapier Manager, Storage, Tables and Forms. Failed actions are not billed either. Leave all of those out of the two fields above.

n8n bills the whole run once, so node count does not change the number. Nothing to exclude.

Make bills one credit per module action, and the trigger module is billable, which the calculator adds for you. Routers and error handlers are free, and filters sit on connections rather than being modules, so leave those out too.
